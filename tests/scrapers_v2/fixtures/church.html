Welcome to Fixture Community Church

We meet every Sunday at ten in the morning for worship and a message from Scripture. Our community welcomes neighbors of all ages to share a meal, serve one another, and build lasting friendships. Children have their own supervised activities during the service. Small groups gather during the week to study the Bible and care for families in our town. Visitors can find accessible parking beside the main entrance.

Our congregation supports the local food pantry and hosts a community dinner each month. You are welcome to join us and learn more about our beliefs and ministries.
